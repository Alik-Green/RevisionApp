
# Edexcel A Level Further Maths (9FM0)

Original revision cards for the four units named in the specification's pure
pathway: Core Pure 1, Core Pure 2, Further Pure 1 and Further Pure 2. Nothing is
copied from a past paper, mark scheme, textbook or the specification itself, and
every card is marked `reviewStatus: AI_UNREVIEWED`.

Maths is written as LaTeX inside `$...$`. The app renders it through its
`RichTextRenderer`, which turns `$\frac{a}{b}$` into `(a)/(b)`, `x^{2}` into a
real superscript and `\theta` into `theta`, and the answer checker normalises the
same way before comparing.

`"structureVerified": false`. The specification could not be downloaded from the
sandbox this content was written in.

## To check against the published 9FM0 specification

- The unit codes and titles. Core Pure 1 and Core Pure 2 are WFM01 and WFM02,
  but the two units called "Further Pure 1" and "Further Pure 2" here are the
  names given by whoever requested this pack, not necessarily Edexcel's own
  naming, and they may correspond to different papers in the real specification.
- Which sub-topic sits in which unit. The allocation below is a reasonable
  split of the pure content but has not been verified, and several topics
  legitimately appear in more than one unit.
- Whether any sub-topic is examined at all, and to what depth. Polar curves,
  hyperbolic functions and the Newton-Raphson method in particular move between
  specifications.
- Point-level references: `specRef` here names the unit and sub-topic in words
  rather than quoting a clause number, because inventing clause numbers would be
  worse than leaving them out.

## Structure

Maths > Further Maths > unit > sub-topic. Twelve leaf sub-topics with twelve
cards each, 144 in total: roots of polynomials, partial fractions, matrices and
determinants, complex numbers and De Moivre, eigenvalues and eigenvectors,
differential equations, summation of series, polar curves, proof by induction,
numerical methods, hyperbolic functions and further integration.

Cards 0001 to 0005 in each file are the original short recall and definition
cards. Cards 0006 to 0012 were added later and are pitched at the multi-step end
of the topic: identities proved from first principles, results that need a
substitution or a factorisation to unlock them, and questions that ask for the
reasoning behind a method as well as the answer. Every numerical answer in this
pack was checked against an independent symbolic calculation before it was
written in.

Definitions and theorems are `TEXT` with key points, worked results are
`NUMERIC` with a tolerance, and formulae are `EXPRESSION` so they can be compared
structurally. Proofs and method questions are `SELF_GRADE`, because "state the
three stages of induction" should be judged against the model answer rather than
scored by token overlap.

Tags: `board = Edexcel`, `level = A Level`, `subject = Further Maths`, plus a
`paper` group with one tag per unit so a whole unit can be selected at once.
