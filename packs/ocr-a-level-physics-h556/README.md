
# OCR A Level Physics (H556)

Original revision cards covering the five content modules of OCR A Level Physics A.
Nothing here is copied from a past paper, a mark scheme, a textbook or the
specification: the questions are written from scratch and every card is marked
`reviewStatus: AI_UNREVIEWED`.

`"structureVerified": false` - the module and sub-topic hierarchy, and every
`specRef`, were written from memory because the specification could not be
downloaded from the sandbox this content was authored in.

## To check against the published H556 specification

- The module numbering and titles (2 Foundations, 3 Forces and motion,
  4 Electrons waves and photons, 5 Newtonian world and astrophysics,
  6 Particles and medical physics).
- Which module each sub-topic belongs to. In particular: circular motion,
  simple harmonic motion and gravitational fields sit in module 5 here, and
  capacitors and particle physics in module 6.
- The point-level `specRef` values such as `3.1.3` or `5.2.1`. These are
  plausible section numbers, not verified ones.
- Whether depth of treatment matches: for example the escape-velocity and
  gravitational-potential points, and how much of medical physics is examined.
- Module 1 (practical skills) and the medical physics topics in module 6 are
  deliberately not covered yet, because they are better revised with a
  practical write-up than with flashcards.

## Structure

Physics > Module > sub-topic. Card files are one per leaf sub-topic, five cards
each, mixing definitions, equations with units, "explain why" questions, worked
numerics and the misconceptions students most often fall into.

Tags: `board = OCR`, `level = A Level`, `subject = Physics`, plus a `module`
group so a whole module can be selected at once.
