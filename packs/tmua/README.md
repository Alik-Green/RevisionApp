
# TMUA

Original practice cards for the Test of Mathematics for University Admission.
Every question here was written for this pack: nothing is taken from a published
past paper, worked solution or preparation book, and every card is marked
`reviewStatus: AI_UNREVIEWED`.

## Structure

TMUA > Paper 1 (Mathematical Thinking), Paper 2 (Mathematical Reasoning) and
Definitions and concepts.

- **Paper 1** questions are multiple choice on the A Level mathematics the test
  assumes - functions and composition, series, algebraic fractions, probability
  without replacement, logarithms, completing the square, Pythagorean triples and
  indices. Each one has a worked explanation, including what the distractors come
  from, because the fastest way to learn the test is to recognise the trap.
- **Paper 2** questions are the logic and proof half: contrapositive, converse
  and inverse, negating universal and existential statements, negating an
  implication, necessary against sufficient, spotting a proof that has argued the
  wrong direction, and the opening assumption of a proof by contradiction.
- **Definitions and concepts** are short cards for the vocabulary both papers
  need.

## Two things to know before using this pack

Real TMUA questions offer **five** options, A to E. The app's multiple-choice
mode is fixed at four - one correct answer plus three authored distractors - so
each question here has three distractors rather than four. The extra option in a
real paper is usually another near-miss of the same kind.

`"structureVerified": false`. No published syllabus or past paper could be
reached from the sandbox this content was written in, so:

- the split of topics between Paper 1 and Paper 2 follows the published
  description of the two papers (mathematical thinking, then mathematical
  reasoning) but has not been checked against a current specification;
- no `board` tag is used, because the awarding body was not verified here;
- the assumed syllabus for Paper 1 - in particular how much trigonometry,
  calculus and coordinate geometry is in scope - should be checked before this
  pack is used for serious preparation;
- `specRef` names the paper and the skill rather than quoting a clause number.

## Tagging

`subject = TMUA`, and `paper = 1` or `paper = 2`. The definitions cards carry
both paper tags, since that vocabulary is used in either paper. Tags inside the
`paper` group are OR-ed together by the app, so selecting one paper narrows the
session to it and selecting both widens it to the whole test.
