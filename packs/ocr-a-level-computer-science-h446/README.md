to cover: 1.2 Software and software development, 1.3 Exchanging data,
2.1 Computational thinking and 2.3 Algorithms. Nothing is copied from a past
paper, mark scheme, textbook or the specification, and every card is marked

## Structure

Computer Science > Component > specification section > sub-topic, giving four
levels of depth. Ten leaf sub-topics with five cards each.

Pseudocode and trace questions are SELF_GRADE, where the model answer is shown
and the student judges their own working, or MCQ where a single wrong step makes
a clearly wrong option. Definitions are TEXT with must-include key points, and
numeric results such as the number of comparisons binary search needs are
NUMERIC with a tolerance.
